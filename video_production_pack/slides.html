01. Problem Understanding
Scene 2 / 5 · 00:35 - 01:10

The Smart Campus Energy Challenge

BUP operates a microgrid with rooftop solar generation, battery energy storage, and dynamic grid tariffs. Schedules must satisfy strict physics while responding to informal human notes.

Human Operator Notes

  • • Informal Wording: "Panel cleaning from noon until 2 PM"
  • • Implicit Calculations: "Keep at least 50% capacity stored"
  • • Realistic Distractors: "Cafeteria menu changes tomorrow" (must map to no_op)
  • • Window Conventions: Start-inclusive, end-exclusive hours

Physical Microgrid Constraints

  • • Hourly Energy Balance: Grid + Solar + Discharge = Demand + Charge
  • • Battery Bounds: Minimum reserve ≤ Energy ≤ Capacity
  • • End-of-Day Neutrality: Final energy == Initial energy
  • • Zero Waste: Mutual exclusivity of charging & discharging
Key Goal: Convert language to structured directives, then find globally minimum grid cost Tolerance: 0.01 BDT / kWh

2-Stage Linear Program (HiGHS)

Formulated with 120 decision variables and 48 physical equality/inequality constraints for the 24-hour horizon.

Stage 1: Cost Minimization

min Σ (grid[h] * tariff[h])

Finds globally minimal grid purchase cost Z* across all 24 hours under active directive constraints.

Stage 2: Peak Grid Smoothing

min M s.t. grid[h] ≤ M
and Cost ≤ Z* + 1e-4

Locks minimal cost and minimizes peak intake M. Prevents transformer stress and erratic battery wear.

Physical Invariants

✔ Energy balance: error < 0.01 kWh
✔ Solar curtailment: s[h] ≤ solar[h]
✔ Exclusivity: charge * discharge = 0
✔ Neutrality: E[23] == E[0]
Solver: SciPy 1.17+ with interior-point & dual simplex Execution Speed: 2.8 ms
